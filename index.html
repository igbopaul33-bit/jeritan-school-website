About Our School

Building a Legacy of Knowledge & Character in Ekureku

Jeritan International School was founded with a clear vision: to bring top-tier, international-standard education to Ekureku, Abi Local Government Area, and surrounding communities in Cross River State.

We combine a rigorous academic curriculum with character formation, ensuring that our students excel not only in examinations like BECE, WAEC, and NECO, but also develop into responsible, innovative future leaders.

Academic Divisions

Comprehensive Learning Pathways

From early foundation to senior secondary graduation, we tailor our teaching methods to each developmental stage.

Foundation Stage

Nursery & Primary School

Focuses on child development, literacy, numeracy, creative arts, and social communication skills in a safe, fun, and modern learning setup.

  • Montessori-inspired Early Childhood Learning
  • Phonics & Expressive Communication
  • Elementary Mathematics & Science Skills
  • Computer Literacy from Early Grade

Key Highlights

Interactive classrooms, engaging educational play areas, and personalized teacher attention to ensure every child builds a rock-solid academic foundation.

Ages: 3 - 11 Years | Standard Nigerian Curriculum
Middle Years

Junior Secondary School (JSS 1 - JSS 3)

Prepares students for basic certificate examinations with a strong emphasis on core sciences, humanities, basic technology, and digital literacy.

  • Mathematics & Integrated Science
  • Introductory Technology & Computer Science
  • Social Studies, Civic & National Values
  • BECE Examination Preparation

Skill Building

Focus on critical thinking, practical science practicals, introductory coding, public speaking, and sports activities.

Grades: JSS 1 to JSS 3 | Examination: BECE / Junior WAEC

Science Stream

We make enrolling your child straightforward and transparent.

1

Submit Application

Entrance Exam
